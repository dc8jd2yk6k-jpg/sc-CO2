# References

Keys are cited as `R#` in the documents and in [`../data/benchmarks.csv`](../data/benchmarks.csv). Where a page or article number could not be confirmed, only journal and year are given; check the primary source before quoting it. Preprints are marked; their results have not been independently reproduced.

## Iron-chalcogenide superconductors

- **R1** M. Z. Shi *et al.*, "FeSe-based superconductors with a superconducting transition temperature of 50 K", *New J. Phys.* (2018). (TBA)₀.₃FeSe made electrochemically in TBA-Br/DMF; ≈ 50 K onset. Related CTA⁺ ≈ 45 K and TMA⁺ ≈ 43 K values are summarised in later reviews.
- **R2** µSR study of (TBA)₀.₃FeSe: nodeless multigap superconductivity, *Phys. Rev. B* **110**, 134504 (2024); arXiv:2410.04781.
- **R3** M. Burrard-Lucas *et al.*, *Nat. Mater.* **12**, 15 (2013). Li/NH₃-intercalated FeSe, ≈ 43 K.
- **R4** T. P. Ying *et al.*, *Sci. Rep.* **2**, 426 (2012). AₓFe₂Se₂ (A = Li, Na, Ba, Sr, Ca, Yb, Eu) via liquid NH₃, 30–46 K.
- **R5** A. Krzton-Maziopa *et al.*, *J. Phys.: Condens. Matter* **24**, 382202 (2012). Li/pyridine-intercalated FeSe, ≈ 45 K.
- **R6** T. Hatakeda *et al.*, *J. Phys. Soc. Jpn.* **82**, 123705 (2013); arXiv:1408.0346. Li/ethylenediamine-intercalated FeSe, ≈ 45 K.
- **R7** X. F. Lu *et al.*, *Nat. Mater.* **14**, 325 (2015). (Li₀.₈Fe₀.₂)OHFeSe, ≈ 40 K; hydrothermal and ion-exchange single crystals reach ≈ 42 K (arXiv:1502.04688).
- **R8** J. P. Sun *et al.*, *Nat. Commun.* (2018). Tc above 50 K re-emerges in (Li₁₋ₓFeₓ)OHFe₁₋ᵧSe near 12.5 GPa (SC-II).
- **R9** T. Noji *et al.*, *Physica C* (2014), and Hatakeda *et al.* (2016). Tc vs interlayer spacing d in intercalated FeSe: roughly linear rise for 5–9 Å, saturating near 45 K. Later data (HMDA, d = 16.2 Å, 38 K) suggest a decline beyond about 12 Å.
- **R10** S. Medvedev *et al.*, *Nat. Mater.* **8**, 630 (2009). FeSe ≈ 37 K at ≈ 9 GPa.
- **R11** Q.-Y. Wang *et al.*, *Chin. Phys. Lett.* **29**, 037402 (2012); S. He *et al.*, *Nat. Mater.* **12**, 605 (2013); S. Tan *et al.*, *Nat. Mater.* **12**, 634 (2013). Monolayer FeSe/SrTiO₃.
- **R12** J. J. Lee *et al.*, *Nature* **515**, 245 (2014). Replica bands; interfacial phonon coupling in FeSe/SrTiO₃.
- **R13** Y. Miyata *et al.*, *Nat. Mater.* **14**, 775 (2015). K-dosed FeSe surfaces.
- **R14** B. Lei *et al.*, *Phys. Rev. Lett.* **116**, 077002 (2016). Gate-tuned FeSe flakes, Tc up to about 48 K.
- **R15** Y. Cui *et al.*, *Chin. Phys. Lett.* (2019). Protonation of FeSe and other layered materials by ionic-liquid gating; HₓFeSe Tc ≈ 41–44 K.
- **R16** J. Guo *et al.*, *Phys. Rev. B* **82**, 180520(R) (2010). KₓFe₂Se₂, ≈ 30 K.
- **R62** S. J. Vivanco and E. E. Rodriguez, "The intercalation chemistry of layered iron chalcogenide superconductors", *J. Solid State Chem.* (2016); arXiv:1603.02334. Review.
